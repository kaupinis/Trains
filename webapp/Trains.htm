Quick Hints

Eightolives' Trains displays the schedules of many trains, subways, and buses. Real-time estimates (in blue) are provided when available. You can view schedule by stop, schedule by trip or schedules by bus route. A map view lets you track a trip or select a nearby stop. You can find itineraries for getting from point A to point B. Audio Mode can assist by talking you through all operations using 5 floating buttons.

Clicking most items will display popup menus with more options. Click the stop location under the time display to change stops or see other platforms at this stop. Stop locations with an * indicate there are other platforms / carriers at this stop. Click any trip listing in the timetable to see the trip schedule.
